About Yuti

Yuti Jetani is a New York based product designer, jewelry designer of Yuni Ratnam, and a product developer at Kira Jewels. She believes in creating high-end products that are comfortable and give a luxurious experience — pieces that offer a sense of belonging and confidence, blending traditional elements with futuristic aesthetics. An animal lover connected to nature, she is always finding new ways to be sustainable and use vegan-friendly materials.

"Every piece of art I create holds a piece of me forever."
